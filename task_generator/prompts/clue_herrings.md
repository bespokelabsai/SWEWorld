## What to write

One or two remarks in which somebody states the earlier decision as settled fact.

It was genuinely settled at the time and genuinely wrong in the end, and the person
saying it believes it. **Never signal that it will be revisited** — no "for now", no
"until we", no "temporary", no hedging of any kind. Somebody recording a decision
does not know it is about to be overturned.

One sentence, two at the absolute most, under thirty words, in that person's voice.
