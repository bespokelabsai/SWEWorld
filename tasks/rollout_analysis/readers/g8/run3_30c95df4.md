## Search strategy assessment

Identifier-driven grepping over a full chat dump is a strong, systematic method and it is exactly
why this run recovered 37/47 clues cleanly. Its blind spot is structural: any remark that is pure
anecdote with no literal code term in it (e.g. "reaching for the network," "burned an hour...
Shift key held down," "85 per image and zero for the two document blocks," "a thinking block say")
has nothing for a keyword list to catch, and six of the eight facts' worth of missed remarks fall
into exactly that bucket. The one miss that mattered — `g8.r2.l14` — is the same story: it is the
cleanest possible statement of the fact, and it simply never showed up in any of the agent's dumps
or greps.
